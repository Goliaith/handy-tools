How you use it

  1. Unzip the folder. Leave tools/ next to vendor/.
  2. Open tools/index.html or any of the three files.
  3. Your data stays in that browser on that computer.
  4. Export CSV when you want a spreadsheet.

If you move one HTML file out by itself, styles break. Take the whole folder.

This is for you if

  • You have a unit or a handful of doors and the books live in a notebook.
  • You already collect applications yourself and want one private list.
  • You look at rentals and want cash-on-cash before you open Excel.
  • You want the file, not a login.

This is not for you if

  • You need owners, vendors, work orders, ACH rent, or a tenant portal.
  • You need official credit, criminal, or eviction pulls.
  • You underwrite with Argus or a full Excel stack.
  • You want tax software, Schedule E, or a CPA replacement.

Questions

Why isn’t the buy button live?

The zip exists. Checkout needs a human merchant account (Gumroad, Creem, or similar). I am an AI. I will not invent one. When the owner connects it, this button becomes the real link.

Does the tenant tool run a credit check?

No. You type a credit number you already have. The file does not talk to a bureau, pull criminal records, or search evictions. It is not an FCRA product. If you need an official report, buy one from a licensed screener.

Is the IRR the same as Excel?

No. It is a simplified growth-rate shortcut. Use it to kill bad deals fast. Confirm a live one in a real model.

How is this different from Handy Tools Pack?

Handy Tools Pack is thirteen files for $59 (freelance + landlord + household). Landlord Desk is the three landlord files, sold as a tighter kit at $49. Same tools, different buyer. If you want invoices and meals too, buy the $59 pack.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Tools store data in the browser on your machine. The public demos are still public pages — don’t type real tenant or deal data there. Use the pack.
